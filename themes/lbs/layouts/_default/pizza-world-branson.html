Pizza World Branson Summer Social Campaign

This page outlines the campaign message, ad copy, recommended targeting, and budget structure for the Pizza World Branson social campaign.

Order Online Focus Tourist + Local Targeting Facebook + Instagram No-index Client Preview

Campaign Angle

Core message

Branson’s Original Gourmet Pizza gives the campaign a clear local identity while still appealing to visitors looking for an easy meal during their trip.

The ad should push one simple action: Order Online.

Recommended Ad Copy

Primary Text Option 1

Branson’s Original Gourmet Pizza is ready when you are.
Order online from Pizza World Branson and enjoy loaded gourmet pizzas, wings, subs, salads, pasta, and more — perfect for family nights, vacation meals, and easy pickup after a day on the strip.

Primary Text Option 2

Hungry in Branson?
Skip the wait and order Pizza World online. Gourmet pizzas, fresh toppings, wings, subs, pasta, and crowd-pleasing favorites made for locals, families, and visitors.

Primary Text Option 3

Pizza night just got easier.
Pizza World Branson makes it simple to order online and feed the whole group with gourmet pizza, wings, subs, salads, and more.

Headline Options

  • Order Pizza World Online
  • Branson’s Original Gourmet Pizza
  • Gourmet Pizza in Branson
  • Pizza, Wings, Subs & More
  • Easy Online Ordering in Branson

Description Options

  • Order online for fast, easy pickup.
  • Serving Branson families, visitors, and groups.
  • Gourmet pizza, wings, subs, pasta, salads, and more.
  • A simple dinner option while you are in Branson.

Recommended Campaign Setup

Objective

Drive online orders and high-intent traffic from people already in or near Branson.

Placements

Facebook Feed, Instagram Feed, Facebook/Instagram Stories, Reels, and local mobile placements.

Call to Action

Order Online should be the primary CTA across the campaign.

Creative Direction

Use the finished pizza creative as the main ad image, emphasizing variety, appetite appeal, and easy online ordering.

Targeting Recommendation

The targeting should be built around Pizza World’s Branson locations and the strongest tourist corridors nearby.

Primary Location Targets

  • 225 Cross Creek Blvd, Ste. F, Branson, MO 65616
  • 3600 W. 76 Country Blvd, Branson, MO 65616
  • 21 Stillwater Trail, Branson, MO 65616

Radius Strategy

  • Start with a tight local radius around each location.
  • Expand along the 76 Country Blvd tourist corridor.
  • Include nearby resort, attraction, and family travel areas.

Audience Signals

  • People currently in Branson or recently in the area.
  • Families, vacationers, entertainment visitors, and local residents.
  • Food, pizza, dining out, family activities, and travel interests.

Timing

  • Heavier delivery Wednesday through Sunday.
  • Prioritize lunch, dinner, and evening ordering windows.
  • Allow Meta to optimize within the daily budget.

Why This Works

  • Clear identity: “Branson’s Original Gourmet Pizza” gives the ad a local hook.
  • Simple action: “Order Online” removes friction and tells people exactly what to do.
  • Strong food visual: The creative shows variety, not just one product.
  • Location relevance: Targeting focuses on people close enough to actually order.
