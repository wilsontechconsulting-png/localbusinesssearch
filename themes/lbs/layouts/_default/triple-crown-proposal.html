This proposal is intended to provide a general overview of how our services are structured. We have not yet discussed your final advertising budgets, so any advertising spend figures shown below are examples only and are intended to demonstrate how the pricing model works.

The goal is complete transparency regarding website development, advertising management, infrastructure, hosting, email delivery systems, and ongoing support so you clearly understand where your investment is being allocated.

Initial Retainer

$5,000

To begin services, an initial retainer of $5,000 is required. This retainer allows us to immediately begin planning, development, setup, implementation, and marketing activities without delays associated with project-by-project billing.

The retainer is applied toward:

  • Website design and development
  • Marketing strategy and planning
  • Search engine optimization setup
  • Google Ads setup and configuration
  • Social media advertising setup
  • Conversion tracking & analytics configuration
  • Email marketing & CRM integration
  • Marketing infrastructure setup
  • Consultation and project management
  • Technical implementation and deployment

Once the retainer balance has been exhausted, services transition to the recurring monthly structure outlined below.

Google Ads Management

20% of ad spend

Transactional Email Infrastructure

$20–$40/month

We use Resend as our transactional email provider to ensure contact form submissions, lead notifications, and appointment requests are delivered reliably — not routed to spam.

  • SPF, DKIM, and DMARC authentication support
  • Improved deliverability & reduced spam filtering
  • Reliable contact form & lead notification delivery
  • Appointment request & system email delivery

Resend costs are billed directly through the platform ($20–$40/month depending on usage). No additional management fee is charged for this service. Accounts are established under client ownership whenever possible.

Additional Development & Enhancement

$65/hour

The monthly plan covers ongoing maintenance, monitoring, security, and standard support. New development, features, integrations, or significant content additions beyond normal maintenance are billed separately at $65/hour.

AI-Assisted Development Efficiency

Our development process uses modern AI-assisted workflows that often allow projects to complete significantly faster than traditional methods. Rather than fixed project fees, we bill only for actual time required.

Example How billable time accumulates
  • 15-minute website update
  • 20-minute content modification
  • 25-minute tracking adjustment

These would be combined into a single billable hour. We do not bill in quarter-hour or half-hour increments. Billing occurs only after cumulative tracked work exceeds one hour.

Examples of Billable Requests

  • New landing pages
  • Additional website pages
  • New forms or integrations
  • CRM & automation workflows
  • Reporting dashboards
  • Appointment scheduling integrations
  • New website functionality
  • Content restructuring
  • Custom coding requests
  • AI-powered features

Any project expected to exceed two billable hours will be discussed and approved prior to work beginning.
