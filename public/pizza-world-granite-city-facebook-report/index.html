Pizza World Granite City

Facebook & Instagram campaign delivery report for Ryan and the Granite City, Illinois location.

Campaign Window April 14 – May 16, 2026 Meta placement data through May 16

Ryan, here’s the short version.

The campaign delivered across Meta’s video and Reels ecosystem, including Facebook Reels, Instagram-connected Reels placements, and Facebook in-stream Reels inventory. The goal was awareness and local visibility — keeping Pizza World in front of people while they were watching short-form video content across Meta placements.

Total Approx. Impressions 27,172 Across reported Meta publisher placements

CMO Readout

What this tells us

The campaign leaned heavily into video-based awareness inventory, which is exactly where Meta can create efficient local reach. For a restaurant, this matters because the buying decision is visual and timing-driven: people see food, remember the brand, and are more likely to choose it when they are hungry or planning a meal.

  • Strongest delivery came from Reels and in-stream video inventory.
  • Meta placed the campaign around a wide mix of entertainment, news, sports, food, and local-interest content.
  • The campaign had meaningful local/regional adjacency, including St. Louis, Metro East, sports, and news placements.
  • No Audience Network delivery was reported, which kept the campaign concentrated inside higher-visibility Meta video placements.

Recommendation

What I’d do next

1. Keep Reels in the mix

Reels drove a major share of reported impressions and should stay in the placement strategy.

2. Add more food-first creative

Pizza, wings, specials, and family meal visuals will give Meta more angles to test.

3. Tighten around local buyers

Layer in Granite City, Metro East, lunch/dinner windows, and local offer messaging.
