What's Included in Management

The 20% management fee is straightforward — there's no large agency retainer, no hidden platform fees. Here's what it covers:

  • Campaign setup and initial ad build
  • Audience targeting (geo, demographic, interest-based)
  • Ongoing monitoring and budget optimization
  • Ad creative guidance and copy (using your content)
  • Brief weekly or biweekly updates via email or text
  • Monthly spend summary with performance notes
  • Campaign adjustments based on what's working
Ad creative production — when we build or design new ad creatives for your campaign — is billed at $65/hour and is drawn from your retainer until exhausted. Additional services outside standard campaign management — such as advanced reporting, landing page builds, Google Ads, or CRM integrations — can be added à la carte as needed.

Organic Social Management (Optional Add-On)

If you'd like us to also handle your organic posts — content scheduling, caption writing, and account management on Facebook and Instagram — that's something we can layer in. Pricing for organic management depends on posting frequency and whether we're creating content or working from yours.

Your answers to the questions above will help us price that accurately. For now, the numbers on this page are advertising-only.

Getting Started

There is a $250 setup fee to kick off the campaign. This covers initial account access, campaign structure, targeting setup, and ad preparation.

The setup fee and monthly management fees will be drawn from your retainer until the retainer is exhausted. Ad spend is billed separately and is not drawn from the retainer — ad spend is paid directly and invoiced at the end of each month based on actual spend.

Payments by credit card include a 4% processing fee.
