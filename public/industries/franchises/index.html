The Challenge

Every Location Is Operating Differently

You have brand standards. But execution is chaos:

  • × Inconsistent follow-up across locations
  • × No centralized reporting (can't compare locations)
  • × Each franchisee using different tools
  • × Strong locations subsidize weak ones with no visibility

You need unified systems that execute consistently across every location.

Solutions

How We Help

Centralized Marketing

Corporate controls campaigns, ad creative, and budget allocation. Franchisees benefit automatically.

→ Brand consistency enforced.

Location-Based Routing

Leads routed to the nearest location automatically. No manual sorting.

→ Every location gets its fair share.

Unified Reporting

Compare performance across locations. See which are thriving and which need help.

→ Data-driven franchise management.

Automated Follow-Up

Every lead gets instant AI response regardless of which location owns it.

→ No leads slip through the cracks.

Franchisee Dashboards

Each owner sees their location's performance. Corporate sees everything.

→ Transparency builds accountability.
