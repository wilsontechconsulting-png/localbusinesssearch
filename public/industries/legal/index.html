The Challenge

Speed Wins Cases

In personal injury and family law, the first firm to respond usually wins the case:

  • × 4-8 hour response times (they've signed with someone else)
  • × Manual intake calls taking 15 min/lead
  • × No qualification system (wasting time on bad cases)
  • × Can't track ROI by practice area or channel

First to respond wins. Every hour you wait, conversion drops 10%.
