📊 Click-Through Rate (CTR)

CTR measures how often people click an ad after seeing it. For restaurants, 3–6% is typical — Pizza World's 6.85% indicates the creative and targeting are highly relevant to local searchers.

💰 Cost Per Click (CPC)

CPC is the average cost to earn one website visit from an ad. At $0.35 per click, Pizza World is paying a fraction of the $0.75–$2.50 industry norm, stretching every advertising dollar significantly.

🎯 Conversion Rate

Conversion rate measures the percentage of ad clicks that result in a valuable action (call, direction request, order). At 30.49%, this is 2x–6x the typical restaurant benchmark of 5–15%.

📍 Cost Per Conversion

The cost to generate one customer action. Pizza World's $1.16 per conversion is dramatically below the $5–$20+ restaurant industry average, representing exceptional return on ad spend.

07 CMO Recommendations

✅ Immediate Actions

Execute within 30 days
  • Increase Google daily budget to $20–$30/day per location to capture more search volume
  • Add retargeting campaign targeting website visitors who did not convert
  • Launch a branded search campaign to capture customers searching "Pizza World Branson"
  • Optimize Google Business Profile with recent photos, updated hours, and menu items
  • Request reviews from recent customers via text/email follow-up

📈 Medium-Term Growth

Execute within 90 days
  • Launch Facebook engagement campaign alongside awareness to generate page likes and social proof
  • Implement SMS/email marketing to capture repeat customer data
  • Build a seasonal promotion campaign for summer tourist season (June–August)
  • Introduce a coupon or offer-based ad creative to drive urgency and first-time orders
  • Test video ad creative on Facebook/Instagram Reels for higher engagement

🚀 Long-Term Growth

6–12 month roadmap
  • Launch a customer loyalty program with digital tracking
  • Build email list through online ordering and in-store incentives
  • Expand to Google Display and YouTube for awareness at scale
  • Launch seasonal campaigns around Branson tourism peaks (summer, fall foliage, holidays)
  • Consider influencer partnerships with Branson travel and food accounts
