# Privacy Policy

Local Business Search respects the privacy of visitors, prospects, and clients. This page explains the basic information we may collect and how we use it.

## Information We Collect

We may collect information you submit through contact forms, consultation requests, email, phone calls, scheduling tools, or other direct communication. This may include your name, business name, email address, phone number, website, message details, and related business information.

We may also collect basic website analytics, such as page visits, traffic sources, device type, browser information, and interaction data. This information helps us understand site performance and improve the experience.

## How We Use Information

We use information to respond to inquiries, provide services, improve our website, evaluate marketing performance, and communicate about relevant business needs.

We do not sell personal information. We may use trusted service providers for website hosting, analytics, communication, scheduling, advertising, automation, or reporting when those tools are needed to operate the business.

## Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent through the contact page.
